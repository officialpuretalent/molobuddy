## Ownership boundaries

- The brand platform owns Molo's promise, personality, language principles and master visual direction.
- The glossary owns canonical product terms and their boundaries.
- The system architecture owns the cross-cutting technical shape and points to the specialised API, application, backend and data designs.
- Specialised design directories may add detail, but they must not silently redefine these project-wide decisions.
